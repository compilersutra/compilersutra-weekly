# CompilerSutra Weekly — #001, November 7 2026

**What changed in compilers this week.**

Welcome to issue **#001** of CompilerSutra Weekly, a Saturday newsletter covering developments across LLVM, Clang, GCC, MLIR, AI compilers, GPU compilers, and related toolchains.
